# Code of Conduct

The standard here is simple: be respectful and constructive.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and bad-faith behavior are not
welcome in issues, pull requests, or any other project space. Assume good
intent, keep feedback about the work rather than the person, and help keep this
a place people are comfortable contributing to.

Concerns can be reported to sunnypatel124555@gmail.com. Reports are handled
confidentially. I reserve the right to remove comments, reject contributions, or
block accounts that breach this standard.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
